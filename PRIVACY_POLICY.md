# Privacy Policy for SeatIck

**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

SeatIck ("the App") is a seating-puzzle game. This policy explains what information the App does — and does not — collect.

## Summary

SeatIck does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App has no account system, no internet connectivity, no analytics, no advertising, and does not share data with any third party.

## Information We Collect

**None.** SeatIck does not collect any personal data, usage data, device identifiers, or location information, and it does not connect to the internet.

## Information Stored On Your Device

To let you track your progress, the App saves the following directly on your device, using Apple's standard local storage (`UserDefaults`):

- Puzzle completion status, star ratings, and best move counts
- Your daily-puzzle streak
- Your selected language and region preference
- Your sound on/off preference

This information never leaves your device. It is not linked to your identity, is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and is removed automatically if you delete the App.

## Sharing

If you choose to share a result card, the App creates an image on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you start it, and the App never sends the image anywhere itself. What happens next depends on the app you pick, and is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

## Children's Privacy

SeatIck does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App collects no data at all, it is safe for use by children of any age.

## Third-Party Services

SeatIck does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking technologies.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future — for example, if a future version of the App adds a new feature that uses data — we will update this document and revise the "Last updated" date above.
